Compared only within the same pair of hands, so a card is not credited for the good hands it turns up in, nor blamed for the strong enemy hands it gets used against (a hand trap is used exactly when they have a big turn). every game = never played without it in a comparable game; turn 1 is solved once per enemy hand and reused, so cards used only on turn 1 land here. Most cards score above 0, because a game where more of your deck came out is a game that went well — compare cards with each other. A card you never used is not listed: "not measured", not "bad". It is per enemy deck, and it is a correlation, not a swap test.

how the enemy and your board are played Every turn is a full search from where the game actually stands. Whoever is not searching defends by a script: hand traps first, then negates, then removals; board pieces wait until the attacker controls a monster; follow-up targets are picked by value (its own weakest given up, the attacker's strongest taken); it never chains onto its own effect. That is a decent player, not a perfect one: the script is fixed, and the searching side is free to play around it. Games before 2026-11-11 used a greedy script (first available interruption at the first chance). Turn 2 first checks whether the enemy can simply kill you (a loss), then plays toward its deck's payoff board.
